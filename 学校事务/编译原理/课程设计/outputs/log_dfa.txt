DFA Graph from subset construction
Regex: (?:\d{1,3}\.){3}\d{1,3}
DFA states:
  D0 = {q0}
  D1 = {q1}
  D2 = {q2}
Start: D0
Accept: D2
Transitions:
  D0 -- (?:\d{1,3}\.){3} --> D1
  D1 -- \d{1,3} --> D2
Construction:
  epsilon-closure(qi) is represented as Di.
  move(Di, input) creates the next DFA subset state.
